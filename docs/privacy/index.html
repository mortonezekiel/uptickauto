Privacy policy

Short version: we don't ask for your name, email or an account to read anything.

Last updated October 21, 2026.

What we collect

Uptick has no accounts, comments or sign-up forms, so we don’t collect your name, email address or anything else you’d have to type in. We don’t sell personal information.

Like most websites, our hosting provider and the services we use may automatically log basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you request and the time of the request. We use it to keep the site running and secure.

If you email us, we keep your message and address only as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of corrections and privacy requests.

Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies and similar technologies are small files or identifiers that sites and services store in your browser. Uptick itself doesn’t set cookies to track you. The analytics and advertising services described below may. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, though some features of those services may stop working.

Analytics

Uptick may use Google Analytics to understand which explainers are read and how people find them. Google Analytics uses cookies or similar identifiers to collect usage data, such as pages viewed, approximate location and device information. You can opt out with Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Live visitor count

Uptick may show how many people visited the site in the past hour. To count visits, each page sends a short request to a small counting service we run on Cloudflare. The service mixes your IP address and browser type with a secret key to make a scrambled code, keeps that code for about an hour so each visitor is counted only once, and then deletes it. It doesn’t store your IP address, set cookies or save anything on your device. Cloudflare handles these requests for us under its privacy policy.

Advertising

Uptick may show ads provided by Google AdSense. Google and other third-party vendors use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Uptick and other sites on the internet.

You can choose how ads are personalized for you:

  • Manage or turn off personalized ads in your Google Ad Settings.
  • Opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized ads at aboutads.info.
  • Learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Visitors in the EEA, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we ask for your consent before analytics or advertising services use cookies, local storage or your personal data for ad personalization. You can decline, and you can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the privacy settings link or message on the site. Records of your choice are kept by our consent tool so we can respect it.

Fonts

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address. Google describes how it handles that data in its privacy policy.

Links to other sites

Our explainers link to the sources they rely on, and we have share buttons for social networks. Those sites have their own privacy policies and practices, which we don’t control.

Children

Uptick is a general-audience news site and isn’t directed to children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal information we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to object to how it’s used. Because we don’t collect accounts or sign-up details, we usually hold very little, but you can ask through our contact page. We’ll respond within the time the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
