## Serie A scores for Oct. 24

Lazio finished Saturday alone in first place in Serie A. Kenneth Taylor scored early in the second half and a Parma own goal doubled the lead in a 2-1 win at the Stadio Olimpico, which lifted Gattuso's team to 22 points, according to [Corriere dello Sport](https://www.corrieredellosport.it/news/calcio/serie-a/2026/10/24-152122839/lazio-parma_diretta_serie_a_segui_la_sfida_all_olimpico_il_risultato_live).

The wildest game of matchday 8 was in Naples. Napoli led 2-0 and then 3-2, but Roma drew 3-3 on Leonardo Balerdi's header in the 89th minute, [The Associated Press reported](https://mymotherlode.com/sports/soccer-general-news/11249181/roma-holds-napoli-to-dramatic-draw-and-dortmund-beats-frankfurt-ahead-of-bayern-clash.html). In the two afternoon games, Como beat Sassuolo 2-0 and Bologna won 2-0 in Sardinia.

## Roma rally twice in Naples

Frank Anguissa put Napoli ahead in the 28th minute with a bicycle kick, and Scott McTominay headed in a Matteo Politano cross in the 48th, per [Football Italia](https://football-italia.net/serie-a-napoli-3-3-roma-epic-battle-mctominay/). Those were McTominay's first league goals this season.

Roma then struck twice in five minutes. Matías Soulé scored in the 69th and Balerdi in the 74th to make it 2-2. McTominay restored the lead with a long-range shot in the 83rd, before Balerdi's header hit the post and crossed the line in the 89th.

Napoli argued that Sam Beukema was fouled in the build-up to that goal, Football Italia reported. Coach Massimiliano Allegri was then sent off for dissent. The goal stood after a VAR check, according to [Radio Marte](https://www.radiomarte.it/napoli-roma-3-3-mctominay-rimonta-balerdi/), which also noted that Bryan Cristante hit the post for Roma before halftime.

## Lazio hold on against Parma

Lazio and Parma were level 0-0 at the break. Taylor scored in the 50th minute after a pass from Belahyane, and in the 58th Nuno Tavares sent in a cross that Parma's Diego Carlos turned into his own net, [DAZN reported](https://www.dazn.com/it-IT/news/calcio/highlights-lazio-parma-2-1-taylor-decisivo-gattuso-primo-rivedi-gol-su-dazn/1mfccwo7tq0oh1ib8jay0ajh9d).

Parma did not score until the fifth minute of stoppage time, when Simone Lontani finished a move set up by Fabbian. Corriere dello Sport noted it was the club's first goal under coach Gilardino. Parma have now lost three in a row. According to DAZN, Lazio have won four straight and are still unbeaten, with seven wins and a draw in eight matches.

## Douvikas and Bologna in the early games

Both 3 p.m. matches were scoreless at halftime. At Como, Douvikas then scored twice in the second half for the club's third league win in a row, [Giallorossi.net reported](https://www.giallorossi.net/serie-a-douvikas-trascina-il-como-bologna-corsaro-a-cagliari-i-risultati-delle-15/). [NewsX](https://www.newsx.com/sports/serie-a-roma-rally-to-draw-3-3-at-napoli-as-como-continue-winning-run-after-humbling-manchester-united-286796/) pointed out that the win came three days after Como's Champions League rout of Manchester United, covered in [our explainer on Como's 4-1 win over United](/como-vs-manchester-united-champions-league/).

At Cagliari, Casale headed in a Miranda corner just before the hour. Bernardeschi added a penalty in the 80th minute after Juan Rodríguez handled the ball, according to [Calcio Napoli 1926](https://www.calcionapoli1926.it/serie-a/serie-a-cagliari-sassuolo-como-bologna/). Cagliari have now lost two straight.

The lead is only three points, and Inter have a game in hand. A win over Fiorentina on Sunday would put Inter level with Lazio on 22. Roma are four points off the top and one point behind Inter, with the draw adding a point to their 17. Como are a point behind Roma. Milan could tie Roma on 18 with a win at Udinese.

Further down, the draw left Napoli on 11 points after eight games, 11 behind Lazio. Bologna moved to 8, while Cagliari stayed on 13. Parma still have 4 points from eight matches, and Sassuolo are on 8.
