## Premier League scores for Oct. 24

Manchester City's unbeaten start to the season ended at Villa Park on Saturday. Nicolas Jackson scored twice, and Aston Villa won 2-1, the first league defeat for City since Enzo Maresca took charge, [Sky Sports reported](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/13599231/aston-villa-2-1-man-city-nicolas-jackson-scores-both-villa-goals-as-enzo-maresca-s-100-per-cent-record-ends-in-defeat). City had won all seven of their previous league games.

That opened the door for Arsenal, who beat Everton 2-0 at the Emirates later in the afternoon and drew level with City on 21 points. In the evening game, Tottenham won 2-0 at Chelsea, and earlier Fulham edged Coventry City 1-0. Four matches produced eight goals, and the road teams won two of them.

## Jackson ends City's winning run

Jackson put Villa ahead in the 7th minute after a loose touch from Marc Guehi, according to [the Guardian's report as republished by UA.NEWS](https://ua.news/en/sport/aston-villa-perervala-idealnu-seriiu-manchester-siti-dublem-dzheksona-the-guardian). Haaland headed City level early in the second half, and Jackson restored the lead on the hour.

City pushed for an equalizer. Iliman Ndiaye hit the woodwork, and Villa goalkeeper Zion Suzuki made several important stops, [ANI reported via The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/sports/premier-league-aston-villa-end-man-citys-perfect-run-arsenal-level-with-table-toppers-after-2-0-win-over-everton). It was Villa's first home league win of the season.

Jackson, who arrived from Chelsea in the summer, now has six goals in his past six games. [BBC Sport noted](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/chelsea-reject-watkins-upgrade-jackson-165926078.html) that the win came exactly four years after Unai Emery was hired to manage the club. City have now lost on four straight visits to Villa Park.

## Arsenal draw level at the top

Arsenal scored early from a set piece. Ezri Konsa finished at the back post in the 7th minute for his first goal for the club, with Viktor Gyokeres setting it up, [Sky Sports said](https://skysports.com/football/news/13601411/arsenal-2-0-everton-goals-from-ezri-konsa-and-gabriel-move-the-champions-level-on-points-with-leaders-man-city). Gabriel headed in Ben White's cross in the 68th minute to make it 2-0.

Gabriel's goal came in his 200th Premier League appearance and was his 21st league goal, according to [TNT Sports](https://www.tntsports.co.uk/football/premier-league/2026-2027/live-arsenal-everton_mtc21883327/live-commentary.shtml). The defending champions have won seven of their eight league games. Everton, who also lost 2-0 to Chelsea last weekend, have now gone 14 league visits to Arsenal without a win, Sky reported.

## Spurs win at 10-man Chelsea

The evening London derby was scoreless until the 77th minute. Chelsea goalkeeper Mike Penders punched a Mateus Fernandes corner into his own net, according to [Sky Sports' match report](https://www.skysports.com/football/chelsea-vs-tottenham-hotspur/report/559516) and [NBC Sports](https://www.nbcsports.com/soccer/live/chelsea-vs-tottenham-hotspur-live-updates-score-goals-highlights-stats-2026-october-24). Cole Palmer was shown a second yellow card around the 80th minute, and Mathys Tel added a solo goal in the 87th.

It was Tottenham's first clean sheet at Stamford Bridge since December 1986, both outlets reported. Spurs have won back-to-back games under Roberto De Zerbi after beating Coventry 2-1 on Monday. Chelsea manager Xabi Alonso said afterward, "We win and we lose together," [The Independent reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/cole-palmer-moment-madness-lights-193857150.html).

At Coventry, Bobb's 80th-minute strike was his first Premier League goal for Fulham, per [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/soccer/match/_/gameId/401879255), and gave Alvaro Arbeloa's side a second win in a row, [Sky reported](https://www.skysports.com/football/coventry-city-vs-fulham/report/559517).

## What it means

The top of the table now has two clubs level after eight games:

| Club | Played | Record | Points | Goal difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Manchester City | 8 | 7-0-1 | 21 | +11 |
| Arsenal | 8 | 7-0-1 | 21 | +8 |

Before Saturday, Arsenal trailed City by three points. One result swung that to zero, and City now lead only on goal difference, by three goals. Both clubs have dropped points in exactly one game, so the race is as tight as it can be after eight rounds.

Further back, Chelsea stayed fourth on 13 points, eight behind the leaders. Villa climbed to sixth with 11 points, 10 off the pace, after collecting three wins in eight games. Everton sit ninth on 10 points after back-to-back losses.

Tottenham's two wins in six days lifted them to 9 points. Before Monday's game with Coventry they had 3 points from six games, so the last two results tripled their total.
