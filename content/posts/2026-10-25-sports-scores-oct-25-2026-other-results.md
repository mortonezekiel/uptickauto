## Sports scores for Oct. 25

Kimi Antonelli made the U.S. Grand Prix look easy. The Mercedes driver led from pole at the Circuit of the Americas and won by just over 13 seconds from Ferrari's Charles Leclerc, according to [Motorsport Week's results](https://www.motorsportweek.com/2026/10/25/f1-2026-united-states-gp-results/). It was his ninth win of the season, [the AP reported](https://www.clickondetroit.com/sports/2026/10/25/kimi-antonelli-wins-dominant-us-grand-prix-and-tightens-his-grip-on-historic-f1-title-chase/), but the title has to wait at least one more race.

In Lahore, Shadab Khan rescued Pakistan from 154-9 with an unbeaten 113 to beat England by one wicket. In Europe, Inter, Bayern Munich and Rennes all finished the day in first place. This post collects those results and five tennis finals. ## Antonelli's title has to wait

Antonelli got away cleanly from pole and was never threatened. Russell, his teammate and nearest rival in the standings, finished only eighth, the AP said. Before the race, [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/f1/breaking-news/live/f1-united-states-grand-prix-results-starting-grid-schedule-how-to-watch-best-bets-and-weather-164555058.html) laid out that a win would clinch the title only if Russell finished ninth or worse and Hamilton fifth or worse. Hamilton's fourth place kept the race alive.

The AP noted that the 20-year-old would be the youngest champion the sport has had, and Italy's first title winner since Alberto Ascari won in 1953.

## Shadab's last-wicket rescue

England were bowled out for 241, with Duckett making his fifth ODI century, [The Guardian reported](https://www.aol.co.uk/articles/england-stunned-shadab-khan-miraculous-164949000.html). Pakistan then slipped to 154-9, still 88 short. Shadab, batting with No. 11 Haris Rauf, added 89 unbeaten runs for the last wicket, Rauf contributing 7 of them. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/sport/cricket/2026/10/25/shadab-khans-world-record-century-secures-thrilling-tri-series-win-for-pakistan/) said no last-wicket pair had ever put on more to win an ODI chase. It was also Shadab's first international hundred.

## Late goals reshape the European tables

Inter trailed Fiorentina 1-0 until stoppage time. Marcus Thuram equalized in the first added minute, and Piotr Zielinski converted a penalty after Thuram was fouled, [AFP reported](https://www.arabnews.com/football/inter-rally-late-to-down-fiorentina-and-regain-serie-a-lead-3008241). Milan bounced back from a midweek loss with a 2-0 win at Udinese, [SempreMilan said](https://sempremilan.com/udinese-0-2-ac-milan-match-report).

In Germany, assistant Aaron Danks ran Bayern's bench because head coach Vincent Kompany had collapsed during Friday's training session; [beIN Sports](https://www.beinsports.com/en-asia/football/bundesliga/articles/freiburg-1-3-bayern-munich-upamecano-scores-stunner-as-gnabry-brings-up-bundesliga-century-2026-10-25) said Kompany was in stable condition. Hoffenheim ended Leverkusen's six-game league winning streak with three goals in the final 11 minutes, [AFP via theScore reported](https://www.thescore.com/bund/news/3634619/hoffenheim-score-3-late-goals-to-snap-leverkusens-winning-streak), though Alexander Prass was sent off for removing his shirt while celebrating his goal.

In France, Marseille nearly threw away a four-goal lead, conceding three times from the 77th minute on, per [Sofascore](https://www.sofascore.com/news/marseille-4-3-le-havre-ligue-1-classic-with-a-late-scare).

## What it means

- **F1:** Antonelli has 365 points to Russell's 242 after 18 of 23 rounds, RaceFans reported. His lead grew from 102 to 123 points in one afternoon, with five rounds left.
- **Serie A:** Inter and Lazio share first on 22 points. Roma and Milan have 18, one ahead of Como.
- **Bundesliga:** Bayern and Borussia Dortmund both have 17 points after seven games; Bayern lead on goal difference.
- **Ligue 1:** Rennes lead with 16 points, ahead of Lille and Monaco on 15, per [Sports Infos](https://www.ski-nordique.net/ligue-1-20262027-classement-apres-j8-rennes-prend-la-tete-psg-lyon-pour-le-sommet-de-lundi.6751885-72348.html). Lyon and Paris Saint-Germain each have 14 points from seven games and meet on Monday.
- **Cricket:** Pakistan are into the final. England, 0-3 so far, can still reach the final, but only if Pakistan beat Sri Lanka on Tuesday and England then beat Sri Lanka heavily on Thursday, The Guardian said.
- **Tennis:** Rybakina's fourth title of 2026 is the most on the WTA Tour, [WTATennis.com noted](https://www.wtatennis.com/news/4598517/by-the-numbers-rybakina-wins-ningbo-open-for-tour-leading-fourth-title-of-season). Medvedev defended a title for the first time in his career, per [Tennis Majors](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/at-the-fourth-attempt-medvedev-defends-a-title-for-the-first-time-coming-from-a-set-down-to-beat-basilashvili-in-almaty-864013.html), and Musetti ended a run of seven straight lost finals, [ATPTour.com said](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fritz-musetti-lyon-2026-final-sunday).
