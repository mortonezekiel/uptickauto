## College football scores for Oct. 22

James Madison came to Boone, North Carolina, at 7-0 and went home with its first loss of the season. Appalachian State kicker James London made all four of his field goals, the last a 40-yarder in overtime, and the Mountaineers won 34-31, [AP reported](https://www.espn.com/college-football/recap/_/gameId/401869845).

In Thursday's other two FBS games, Memphis needed overtime to finish a comeback against East Carolina, and Liberty rolled at Kennesaw State to clinch bowl eligibility.

## App State 34, James Madison 31 (OT)

The fourth quarter alone produced 28 points. JMU went up 24-21 on a 47-yard field goal by Morgan Suarez. Then App State's Colton Phares intercepted a pass and ran it back to the JMU 14 with 6:03 left, and two plays later backup quarterback Henry Hasselbeck, the son of former NFL quarterback Matt Hasselbeck according to [Yahoo Sports](https://sports.yahoo.com/college-football/breaking-news/article/appalachian-state-hands-james-madison-its-first-loss-of-the-season-in-34-31-ot-win-030743234.html), found Luke Douglas for a 4-yard score.

JMU answered with a 15-play, 71-yard drive that included a fourth-and-3 conversion, and quarterback Camden Coleman ran in from 6 yards out with 3 seconds left to tie it. In overtime, Suarez's 47-yard attempt bounced off the left upright, which set up London's winner. JMU outgained App State 423-366, according to the [CBS Sports recap](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/gametracker/recap/NCAAF_20261022_JMAD@APLST/), but committed the game's only turnover. Phares finished with 13 tackles, [App State said](https://appstatesports.com/news/2026/10/23/football-londons-ot-kick-caps-dramatic-app-state-win-over-jmu.aspx).

## Memphis 41, East Carolina 34 (OT)

East Carolina led 16-3 at halftime. Memphis scored 17 points in the third quarter, two of them touchdown passes by Stokes, and the teams traded scores until ECU's Mitch Griffis hit Landon Sides for a 20-yard touchdown that made it 34-34. Stokes ran in from 5 yards in overtime, and Ellis Ellis Jr. sealed it with an interception, [Memphis Athletics reported](https://gotigersgo.com/news/2026/10/22/football-tigers-overcome-second-half-deficit-in-41-34-overtime-win-over-east-carolina). Stokes completed 29 passes without an interception.

## Liberty 34, Kennesaw State 6

Liberty scored on its first drive and never let Kennesaw State into the end zone. Deshawn Purdie threw a 56-yard touchdown to Coleman, who later ran one in from 9 yards, [AP reported](https://www.cbssports.com/college-football/news/liberty-routs-kennesaw-state-34-6-behind-jaylon-colemans-two-tds/). It was the second time this season Liberty held an opponent without a touchdown, according to [Liberty Athletics](https://libertyflames.com/news/2026/10/22/football-flames-are-bowl-bound-after-running-win-streak-to-6-in-a-row).

## What it means

- **JMU's playoff path narrows:** Yahoo Sports called the loss a significant blow to the Dukes' hopes of a second straight College Football Playoff trip. It was their first regular-season loss since a 28-14 defeat at Louisville in Week 2 of 2025. They are 7-1, 4-1 in the Sun Belt, and were receiving 52 votes in the AP poll.
- **App State back in the race:** the Mountaineers improved to 5-2, 2-1 in the Sun Belt. JMU's one conference loss now matches theirs.
- **Memphis keeps pace:** the Tigers are 6-2 and 3-1 in the American. East Carolina fell to 3-4 (1-2).
- **Liberty's streak:** six straight wins after a 0-1 start leave the Flames at 6-1. Kennesaw State is 1-6 and has lost six in a row since winning its opener 47-0, AP noted.
