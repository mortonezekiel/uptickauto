## NHL scores for Oct. 25

Montreal won the outdoor Heritage Classic in Winnipeg 2-1, with rookie goaltender Jacob Fowler turning aside 33 shots before a crowd of 32,342 at Princess Auto Stadium, [AP reported](https://www.espn.com/nhl/recap?gameId=401891807). The loss was the Jets' fifth in a row and their first game since sending Connor Hellebuyck to New Jersey.

Across the country, Quinn Hughes collected his 500th NHL point in his first game back in Vancouver, and Minnesota won 4-2 to extend the Canucks' slide to 10 straight. Sunday had five games, matching the schedule, and all five went final in regulation, per the [Hockey-Reference scoreboard](https://www.hockey-reference.com/boxscores/?year=2026&month=10&day=25) and [SportBusy's schedule](https://www.sportbusy.com/nhl-schedule-october-2026/).

## Fowler holds on outdoors

Lane Hutson opened the scoring at 7:19 of the second period, and Kaiden Guhle doubled the lead at 11:04, according to [NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/news/montreal-canadiens-winnipeg-jets-heritage-classic-game-recap-october-25-2026). Fowler kept the Jets off the board until Isak Rosen scored his first of the season late in the third period. Montreal then killed a tripping penalty to Josh Anderson in the final 1:32, [theScore](https://www.thescore.com/news/3635222) noted.

Fowler has won five starts in a row. NHL.com said he is the youngest goalie ever to start an outdoor game and the fourth rookie to win one. Stuart Skinner made 19 saves for Winnipeg, and Dawson Mercer, acquired in the Hellebuyck trade, made his Jets debut. Winnipeg joined Edmonton as the only Canadian clubs to host two outdoor games; the Jets lost the first one 3-0 to the Oilers in 2016.

## Hughes hits 500 in Vancouver

The former Canucks captain was booed each time he had the puck but also got a video tribute in the first period, and he raised his stick to the crowd, the [Parksville Qualicum News](https://saobserver.net/2026/10/25/boos-for-hughes-wild-win-4-2-as-ex-canucks-captain-jeered-in-return-to-vancouver/) reported. His power-play rebound goal at 12:55 of the second made it 3-0 and was point No. 500, in his 520th game. Per the [Wild's recap](https://www.nhl.com/wild/news/minnesota-wild-vancouver-canucks-game-recap-october-25-2026-x2730), only six defensemen got there faster, and among American defensemen only Brian Leetch did.

Hughes then sent Kaprizov in alone for a 4-0 lead. Maxim Shabanov and Bobby Brink scored earlier. Max Sasson and Brock Boeser answered in the third. Calvin Pickard won his first game for Minnesota, and the Wild outshot Vancouver 34-18, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nhl/gametracker/recap/NHL_20261025_MIN@VAN/).

## Predators cash in on the power play

Nashville scored on four of its seven power plays, its first four-goal power-play night since Jan. 7, 2012, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/colorado-avalanche-nashville-predators-game-recap-october-25-2026). Filip Forsberg scored at 4:01 of the first on a Roman Josi setup, and Stamkos added a goal that video review confirmed had crossed the line.

O'Reilly scored twice in the second, sandwiched around a Jack Drury goal, and Nashville led 5-0 before Artturi Lehkonen and Devon Toews scored for Colorado. Stamkos closed it on a 5-on-3. Josi had three assists. Justus Annunen made 19 saves for his first win this season against Scott Wedgewood, the goalie he was traded for last November, [AP noted](https://www.wral.com/news/ap/75ae1-oreilly-stamkos-each-score-twice-as-the-predators-beat-the-avalanche-6-2/). Lehkonen's point streak is at seven games.

Minnesota's 18 points lead the Western Conference, per Hockey-Reference, and the Wild are 6-2-1 over their last nine. Vancouver sits last in the West with five points, 13 behind Minnesota.

In Dallas, the Islanders tied the game three times before Wyatt Johnston broke a 3-3 tie early in the third and Robertson added a breakaway goal, [AP reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/jason-robertson-scores-twice-help-003925101.html). The loss ended New York's five-game point streak, and Matthew Schaefer's 25th career goal moved him past Bobby Orr for second among teenage defensemen, trailing only Phil Housley, the [Islanders said](https://www.nhl.com/islanders/news/takeaways-islanders-five-game-point-streak-ends-in-6-3-loss-to-stars). Dallas has killed 20 straight penalties.

In Chicago, Eller beat Spencer Knight during a Blackhawks line change for his first goal of the season, [NHL.com reported](https://www.nhl.com/news/florida-panthers-chicago-blackhawks-game-recap-october-25-2026). Chicago has three goals in its last three games, all losses, and its power play ranks last in the league at 8.33%, per [CBS Sports](https://www.cbssports.com/nhl/gametracker/recap/NHL_20261025_FLA@CHI/).
