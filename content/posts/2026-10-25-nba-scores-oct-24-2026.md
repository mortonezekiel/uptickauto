## NBA scores for Oct. 24

Denver needed overtime and a 23-point comeback to beat New Orleans 141-135 on Saturday, the wildest of the NBA's eight games. Jamal Murray scored 42, and Nikola Jokic added 35 points, 15 rebounds and 10 assists, according to [Land of Basketball's daily summary](https://www.landofbasketball.com/nba_daily_summary/20261024.htm). It was the only game of the night to go past regulation, and its 276 combined points led the slate.

Philadelphia stayed perfect without LeBron James. Joel Embiid recorded a triple-double in a 127-116 win over Milwaukee, and the 76ers and the Rockets are now the only 3-0 teams after Houston won 121-114 in Atlanta. In Phoenix, Devin Booker scored 43 against a depleted Golden State roster. No game was postponed.

## Denver's long climb back

The Pelicans were in control for most of the night. They shot 65.9% before halftime and led 53-30 in the second quarter, [AP reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/jokic-triple-double-murray-scores-041444616.html). They still led by 19 after three quarters and by eight, 124-116, with 1:37 to play.

Denver closed regulation on a 10-2 burst. Murray drew a foul on a 3-point try and made two of three free throws with 16 seconds left to tie it at 126, [The Ticket reported](https://www.theticket.com/2026/10/24/nuggets-rally-from-23-down-to-force-overtime-beat-pelicans/). Jokic's free throws gave Denver its first lead since the opening quarter, and rookie Trevon Brazile, playing his first home game, hit a 3 for a 133-129 edge. Brazile scored seven of his 17 in the extra period.

Murray and Jokic combined for 77 points, more than half of Denver's total. Jokic's triple-double was the 199th of his regular-season career, second only to Russell Westbrook's 209, per AP. Trey Murphy III led the Pelicans with 26. New Orleans is 0-3 under first-year coach Jamahl Mosley.

## Booker sets a Suns opener mark

Golden State played without Stephen Curry and Draymond Green, according to both [SB Nation](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/game-recap-booker-drops-43-044003248.html) and [Burn City Sports](https://burncitysports.com/2026/10/24/phoenix-suns-warriors-final-booker/), and still led 57-54 at the break. The third quarter flipped it. Phoenix made 16 of its 20 shots in the period, including six of eight from deep, and took a 102-92 lead into the fourth.

Booker went 17 of 25 from the field and added seven rebounds and seven assists. His 43 points are a franchise high for a home opener, SB Nation reported, and they made up about a third of Phoenix's 131. It was the Suns' first win of the season. Melton, Yaxel Lendeborg (23) and Brandon Williams (20) kept the Warriors close.

## Embiid fills the gap

With James sitting out for what the team listed as lower back pain management, Embiid posted 28 points, 11 rebounds and 10 assists, [AP reported](https://abcnews.com/Sports/wireStory/embiid-posts-triple-double-lebron-76ers-perfect-127-137546244). The 10th assist came on a Jaylen Brown 3. Brown, playing on his 30th birthday, scored 27, and Tyrese Maxey had 26, per [NBC Sports Philadelphia](https://www.nbcsportsphiladelphia.com/nba/philadelphia-76ers/observations-after-sixers-beat-bucks-to-move-to-3-0-embiid-posts-triple-double/761259/), which noted it is the second straight season the Sixers have opened 3-0. VJ Edgecombe chipped in 17 points and 13 rebounds. Jamie Jacquez Jr. scored 21 for winless Milwaukee.

## What it means

Three games in, the early standings for Saturday's teams look like this:

- **3-0:** Philadelphia and Houston. The Rockets were 2-0 coming into Atlanta, per [ESPN's game page](https://www.espn.com/nba/game?gameId=401909856), and led 59-48 at halftime. Jalen Johnson's 16 assists could not offset a deficit that reached 20 in the third quarter, [SB Nation reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/hawks-t-keep-rockets-home-032102453.html).
- **2-1:** Atlanta, Charlotte and Washington.
- **1-2:** Chicago, San Antonio and Dallas.
- **0-3:** Milwaukee and New Orleans. Miami also remains without a win.

Three teams got their first win: Cleveland, Phoenix and San Antonio. The Cavaliers nearly gave back a 17-point lead in the last four minutes before Sam Merrill's free throws sealed it, [AP reported](https://www.cbsnews.com/newyork/news/cavaliers-nets-win-home-opener-125-121/). The Spurs answered a blown 14-point lead by opening the fourth on a 17-5 run in Dallas, which played without Cooper Flagg (shoulder) and Kyrie Irving (rest), [AP said](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/short-handed-mavericks-lose-122-025636898.html).

Washington turned a five-point halftime deficit into a 15-point lead by winning the third quarter 40-20, according to the [Yahoo Sports box score](https://sports.yahoo.com/nba/washington-wizards-chicago-bulls-2026102404/). Chicago has made 23% of its 3-point attempts through three games, [AP noted](https://abcnews.com/Sports/wireStory/young-scores-25-points-wizards-hold-off-wilson-137546842). In Charlotte, the Heat erased a 22-point gap and tied it three times before Allen's four-point play with 3:46 left, [AP reported](https://abcnews.com/Sports/wireStory/brandon-miller-scores-34-points-hornets-beat-winless-137545319).
