## What happened

El Clásico went to Real Madrid on Sunday, Oct. 25. Kylian Mbappé scored twice, the second three minutes from the end of regulation, and Madrid won 2-1 at Spotify Camp Nou, according to the live reports from [VAVEL](https://www.vavel.com/en-us/soccer/2026/10/25/1276711-barcelona-vs-real-madrid-live-score-laliga.html) and Belgian broadcaster [Sporza](https://sporza.be/nl/matches/voetbal/buitenland/spa/laliga-ea-sports/2026-2027/regulier/10/fc-barcelona-real-madrid-mbappe-kroont-real-tot-winnaar-van-clasico-franse-superster-bezorgt-barcelona-late-dreun~1788333960856/).

The result ended Barcelona's perfect run in La Liga. Hansi Flick's side had won all nine league games before kickoff, [101 Great Goals](https://www.101greatgoals.com/football/la-liga/barcelona-real-madrid-report-result-goals-kylian-mbappe-raphinha/) and other outlets noted. Barcelona are still top, but the gap to Madrid is now three points instead of six.

## How the Clásico was decided

| Minute | Score | What happened |
|---|---|---|
| 41 | 0-1 | Gerard Martín tries to clear a low ball from Yan Diomandé, and it rebounds off Mbappé into the net |
| 61 | 1-1 | Raphinha finishes low after a pass from Pedri |
| 87 | 1-2 | Jude Bellingham turns and finds Eduardo Camavinga, whose ball sets up Mbappé |

Minute marks follow Sporza and [El Español](https://www.elespanol.com/deportes/futbol/20261025/fc-barcelona-real-madrid-futbol-hoy-directo-alineaciones-resultado-ultima-hora-clasico-liga/1003744434151_10.html); VAVEL's running text logged the winner a minute later.

Barcelona had more of the ball. At halftime they held about 59% possession and had out-shot Madrid 9-6, VAVEL's figures showed. But [El Espectador](https://www.elespectador.com/deportes/futbol-mundial/en-vivo-barcelona-vs-real-madrid-siga-el-minuto-a-minuto-de-el-clasico/) described a home side that kept possession without finding a way through a deep Madrid defense. Andriy Lunin, starting because Thibaut Courtois had the flu, made a key save on a Gerard Martín header midway through the second half.

Both teams lost a defender to injury. Barcelona right back Xavi Espart went off around the half-hour mark, and Madrid's Federico Valverde was replaced by Denzel Dumfries early in the second half. Neither club had announced the extent of either injury in the reports reviewed. No player was sent off.

## Why everyone is talking about it

**Mourinho's return.** Real Madrid rehired José Mourinho in June on a contract through June 30, 2029, [Sky Sports reported](https://www.skysports.com/football/news/11835/13553162/jose-mourinho-to-real-madrid-former-chelsea-and-man-utd-boss-bernabeu-return-confirmed-by-spanish-giants), 13 years after his first spell ended. [TimesLIVE](https://www.timeslive.co.za/sport/soccer/2026-10-24-mourinho-brings-new-edge-to-clasico/) said this was his 18th Clásico as Madrid coach and that his record going in was five wins, six draws and six losses, with only two wins at Camp Nou. Sunday's result evens that ledger at 6-6-6 and gives him a third win in Barcelona.

**A rough week for Barcelona.** Five days earlier, Barcelona lost 4-2 at Paris Saint-Germain in the Champions League. Sunday made it two defeats in a row for a team that had been scoring about 4.3 goals per league game.

**Missing stars.** Madrid played without Vinícius Júnior, who was serving the second game of a four-match suspension, [USA TODAY reported](https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fc-barcelona-vs-real-madrid-180117102.html). Barcelona were without Jules Koundé and Dani Olmo, [World Soccer Talk said](https://worldsoccertalk.com/news/barcelona-vs-real-madrid-live-updates-minute-by-minute-coverage-of-the-2026-27-laliga-season-matchday-10).

## The title race in numbers

The top three after 10 games, from [Goal.com's table](https://www.goal.com/en/laliga/table/34pl8szyvrbwcmfkuocjm3r6t):

| Club | Record (W-D-L) | Goal difference | Points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barcelona | 9-0-1 | +29 | 27 |
| Atlético Madrid | 8-1-1 | +12 | 25 |
| Real Madrid | 8-0-2 | +15 | 24 |

A Barcelona win would have put them nine points clear of Madrid. Instead, three teams sit within three points, with 28 rounds still to play in a 38-game season.

The rivalry itself also tightened its long-term ledger. Before Sunday, Madrid had won 80 La Liga Clásicos to Barcelona's 77, with 35 draws, World Soccer Talk reported. That now becomes 81-77-35 over 193 league meetings. Last season Barcelona took two of the three meetings, including a 2-0 league win on May 10 that clinched the title, according to [Sports Illustrated](https://www.si.com/soccer/real-madrid-vs-barcelona-last-clasico-results).

Yes. Barcelona lead with 27 points from 10 games, two ahead of Atlético Madrid and three ahead of Real Madrid.
