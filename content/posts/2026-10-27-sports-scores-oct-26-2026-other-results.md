## Brewers 10, Rays 1: a challenge that changed the inning

Milwaukee led 2-0 in the fifth when a grounder by William Contreras looked like an inning-ending force-out at second. The Brewers challenged, the call was overturned, and the bases stayed loaded, [Bleacher Report reported](https://bleacherreport.com/articles/25520868-brewers-beat-rays-historic-rally-box-score-stats-highlights-2026-mlb-world-series-game-3). What followed was seven straight two-out hits, which Yahoo Sports called a postseason first. Reliever Cam Booser faced four batters without getting an out. Robert Gasser threw six scoreless innings and allowed one hit, and Jackson Chourio went 4 for 5. Tampa Bay's only run came on Yandy Díaz's double in the ninth off Brandon Woodruff, who was pitching for the first time since a shoulder injury in July. For background on the matchup, see [our World Series explainer](/world-series-2026-rays-vs-brewers/).

## Cowboys 38, Eagles 21: Williams wears them down

Philadelphia took a 21-20 lead early in the fourth quarter on Makai Lemon's first career touchdown. Dallas answered with a CeeDee Lamb touchdown and a Dak Prescott two-point run, then added a Brandon Aubrey field goal and a 2-yard Javonte Williams score after the Eagles failed on fourth-and-9, [the Cowboys' recap said](https://www.dallascowboys.com/news/game-recap-cowboys-run-over-eagles-38-21). Williams finished with 155 yards on 25 carries, a career high. Prescott threw three touchdown passes, two to Lamb. The Eagles are playing without Jalen Hurts, who is out for the season, with Tanner McKee starting, [NBC 5 noted](https://www.nbcdfw.com/news/sports/nfl/cowboys-eagles-score-week-7/4095972/).

## PSG 2, Lyon 0: the bench decides it

The match was scoreless until the 72nd minute, when Senny Mayulu scored seven minutes after coming on, set up by Mika Godts, [Foot Sur 7 reported](https://www.foot-sur7.fr/789889-psg-lyon-notes-ligue-1). Godts, also a substitute, added his first PSG goal in the 84th minute. PSG played without Ousmane Dembélé, Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Marquinhos. Lyon, which started the day in first place, dropped to fifth, [KarloBag reported](https://karlobag.eu/en/sports/psg-vs-lyon-2-0-at-parc-des-princes-late-goals-from-mayulu-and-godts-and-a-rise-j5puh/).

## Rune's return

Holger Rune needed 2 hours, 39 minutes to get past Quentin Halys, who hit 32 aces, but the win was his first at tour level in 373 days, since an Achilles injury in October 2025, [Tennis Majors reported](https://www.tennismajors.com/atp/373-days-after-his-achilles-injury-rune-edges-halys-in-basel-for-first-atp-win-864116.html). In Vienna, Alex de Minaur trailed Tomas Machac by a set and 4-2 before winning, [the ATP Tour said](https://www.atptour.com/en/news/de-minaur-machac-vienna-2026-monday). In Tokyo, defending champion Belinda Bencic withdrew with a right ankle injury, [Just Women's Sports reported](https://justwomenssports.com/reads/alex-alexandra-eala-pan-pacific-open-tokyo-2026/).

## What it means

- **World Series:** Milwaukee needs one win in up to four games. Yahoo Sports notes the Brewers are 10-1 this postseason, and a sweep would make them the first team to sweep both a league championship series and the World Series. Games 4 and 5 are at Tropicana Field; Games 6 and 7, if needed, would be in Milwaukee.
- **NFC East:** Dallas (3-4) moved ahead of Philadelphia (2-5), [the Eagles' recap noted](https://www.philadelphiaeagles.com/news/cowboys-vs-eagles-game-recap-2026-nfl-week-7-monday-night-football-saquon-barkley-dak-prescott). The Cowboys are one win from .500.
- **Ligue 1:** PSG have 17 points after eight matches, one ahead of Rennes, [Paris Fans reported](https://en.parisfans.fr/psg-vs-lyon-the-bench-delivers-the-win-and-top-spot-the-hits-and-the-misses.html), and two ahead of Lille and Monaco, per Foot Sur 7. Lyon sit fifth on 14, three points off the top.
- **La Liga:** Sevilla moved to sixth on 17 points; Osasuna are 12th on 11, [La Nación reported](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/deportes/canchallena/sevilla-vencio-2-0-a-osasuna-por-la-liga-de-espana-nid26102026/).
- **ATP Finals race:** de Minaur's win matters for Turin; the ATP Tour says he is 10th in the live race.
