## Cricket scores for Oct. 27

India wrapped up its T20I series in New Zealand with two games to spare, and it did so in a hurry. Chasing 199 in Wellington, India got there in 13.2 overs, winning by six wickets with 40 balls left, [Outlook India reported](https://www.outlookindia.com/sports/cricket/india-vs-new-zealand-3rd-t20i-live-score-squad-ind-vs-nz-2026-match-today-updates). Abhishek Sharma made 113 from 38 balls.

In Lahore, Sri Lanka needed even less time. Dilshan Madushanka took 5-34, a career best, as Pakistan were bowled out for 110 in 25.3 overs, and Sri Lanka won by eight wickets to book a place in Saturday's tri-series final, [WION reported](https://www.wionews.com/sports/sl-vs-pak-lahore-odi-madushanka-five-wicket-haul-sri-lanka-reach-tri-series-final-england-out-1793107033535). The result knocked England out.

## India sweep past New Zealand in Wellington

New Zealand's 198-3 looked competitive. Daryl Mitchell made 89 not out from 52 balls, with Finn Allen (40 from 25) and Bevon Jacobs (39 not out from 23) helping, according to Outlook India.

It lasted about seven overs. Abhishek and Vaibhav Sooryavanshi, who was moved up to open after batting at No. 3 in the second match, put on 133 for the first wicket in 7.1 overs, [The Herald reported](https://theherald.co.za/sport/cricket/2026-10-27-destructive-abhishek-leads-india-to-series-win-over-new-zealand/). Jacob Duffy's first over cost 26. India were 178-2 after 10 overs. Sooryavanshi made 45 from 19 balls before Kyle Jamieson removed Abhishek, who left to a standing ovation.

## Madushanka sends Sri Lanka to the final

Sri Lanka captain Kusal Mendis won the toss and bowled first, and Pakistan lost three wickets in the powerplay, WION reported. Babar Azam's 29 was the top score, with Hasan Nawaz adding 17; Sahan Arachchige took 2-24 to go with Madushanka's five.

The chase was simple. Kamindu Mendis and Avishka Fernando opened with a 50 stand, and Kamindu finished on 62 not out from 60 balls as Sri Lanka reached 114-2 in 18.1 overs, [according to the CREX scorecard](https://crex.com/cricket-live-score/pak-vs-sl-5th-match-odi-tri-series-in-pakistan-2026-match-updates-13NF). Pavan Rathnayake was there at the end on 23.

## Afghanistan open with a win in Sharjah

Zimbabwe chose to bowl and had Afghanistan at 31-3 after the powerplay, [CricTracker reported](https://www.crictracker.com/cricket-reviews/rashid-khan-ijaz-ahmadzai-power-afghanistan-to-37-run-win-in-1st-t20i-vs-zimbabwe/). Ijaz Ahmadzai's quick half-century and Shahidullah Kamal's 46 rebuilt the innings, and Rashid Khan's 30 from 10 balls lifted it to 177. Newman Nyamhuri took 4-31 for Zimbabwe, [The SportsTak scorecard shows](https://thesportstak.com/cricket/match/afg-vs-zim-1st-t20i-afghanistan-and-zimbabwe-tour-of-uae-27-october-2026-274660/highlights). Ryan Burl's 32 led the reply, but Fazalhaq Farooqi (3-13) and Rashid (2-20) bowled Zimbabwe out for 140.

## What it means

- **The speed of India's chase:** 199 runs from 80 balls works out to almost 15 runs an over, about five an over faster than New Zealand managed. Abhishek's strike rate was close to 300.
- **A short day in Lahore:** the ODI used 43.4 of a possible 100 overs. Sri Lanka won with 31.5 overs, or 191 balls, still to bowl.
- **Tri-series:** Pakistan and Sri Lanka meet in the final on Saturday in Lahore. Pakistan had beaten England by one wicket on Sunday, WION reported, and Sri Lanka's last group game against England is now a dead rubber.
- **NZ vs. India:** India lead 3-0 with two matches left, so the series is decided.
- **Afghanistan vs. Zimbabwe:** Afghanistan lead the three-match series 1-0.

## What's next

- **Friday:** New Zealand vs. India, 4th T20I, at Eden Park in Auckland, The Herald reported.
- **Saturday:** Pakistan vs. Sri Lanka, tri-series final, in Lahore.
- Sri Lanka's remaining group game against England, also at the Gaddafi Stadium, comes before the final.
