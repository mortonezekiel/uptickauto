## What we collect

Uptick doesn't require an account and has no sign-up forms. We don't sell personal information.

Like most websites, our hosting provider may log basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you request, to keep the site running and secure.

## Fonts

Our pages load typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers. Google describes how it handles that data in its privacy policy.

## Analytics and advertising

If we turn on analytics (such as Google Analytics) or advertising (such as Google AdSense), those services may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits or show ads, including personalized ads. When either is turned on, we'll list it here. You can manage personalized ads in your Google ad settings and block cookies in your browser.

## Links to other sites

Our explainers link to the sources they rely on. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page.
