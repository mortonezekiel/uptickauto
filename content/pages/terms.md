## What Uptick is

Uptick publishes explainers about stories that are trending. They are general information, written from published sources and reviewed against them. They aren't legal, medical, financial or other professional advice. For decisions that matter, talk to a qualified professional and use official sources.

## Accuracy

We work to get the facts right and to correct mistakes quickly, but news changes fast and we can't promise that every explainer is complete or current at the moment you read it. Each explainer shows when it was published or last updated and lists the sources it relied on. See our [editorial standards](/editorial-standards/) and [how to report an error](/contact/).

## How content is made

Uptick explainers are researched and drafted with AI tools and written under human-designed editorial rules, including source requirements, copy checks and fact checks. We say so on every explainer. Our artwork is original and generated by our own tools, not taken from news photos or other publishers.

No advertiser, company or campaign pays for coverage, reviews our explainers in advance or influences what we write. Please don't click ads for any reason other than your own interest. Links to other websites are provided so you can check our sources. We don't control those sites and aren't responsible for their content or privacy practices.
